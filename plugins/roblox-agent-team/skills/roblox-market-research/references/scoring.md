# Scoring rubric (orchestrating session)

Score each loop 1-5 on each line, then weight.

| Factor | Weight | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proven earnings | 3 | nothing in the loop plausibly clears the target | several games, including small ones, plausibly clear it |
| Momentum | 2 | falling for weeks | rising across 7d and 30d |
| Competition gap | 3 | leaders polished, many good copies | leaders have clear complaints, few decent copies |
| Solo build cost | 3 | needs big team, heavy content, complex netcode | Phase A in ~2 weeks with greybox |
| Monetization fit | 2 | players hate paying in this genre | natural boosts/passes, leaders sell well |
| Retention structure | 2 | one-and-done | clear session + return loops |
| Trend lifespan | 1 | fad peaking now | evergreen or early |

Max = 80. Anything under 48 doesn't make the top 3.

**Kill rules** (score doesn't matter):
- Depends on someone else's IP or brand.
- Needs constant content drops a solo dev can't sustain.
- Only works because of ad spend or a big YouTuber.
- Money math needs more than ~2,000 avg CCU at "decent" monetization to hit
  the target.
